The Value Cube

Building the Trusted Outcome Economy with Enterprise AI

by Sanjeev Azad

Enterprises can measure what GenAI costs to the cent, and still can't say what it's worth. So they optimize the only number they can see, cutting tokens while quality, trust, and rework quietly erode. The Value Cube is the operating manual for the other way: score the six faces, keep them honest with five cross-cutting dimensions, run the lifecycle, apply the patterns, and refuse to claim a saving until the evidence earns it.
